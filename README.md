# Cybersecurity-Management-Incident-Response-Plan
Western Governors University Performance Assessment - Cybersecurity Management Plan



## Overview

This project evaluates the cybersecurity posture of a fictional
retail organization and develops a comprehensive security improvement
plan based on identified security and compliance gaps.

The project focuses on security governance, risk mitigation,
incident response, business continuity, regulatory compliance,
and cybersecurity awareness.

## Key Areas

- Incident Response Planning
- Security Risk Assessment
- NIST Cybersecurity Guidance
- PCI DSS Compliance
- GDPR Compliance
- Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery
- Vulnerability Remediation
- Security Policies and Procedures
- Security Awareness Training

## Incident Response

Developed an incident response plan covering:

- Preparation
- Detection and Analysis
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery
- Post-Incident Activity
- Incident escalation
- Evidence preservation
- Lessons-learned reviews

## Security Controls & Remediation

Recommended controls included:

- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Principle of Least Privilege
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Encryption of sensitive data
- Patch and vulnerability management
- Centralized security logging and monitoring
- Data retention requirements
- PII and payment-card protections

## Business Continuity

Developed business continuity recommendations incorporating:

- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Geographic redundancy
- Backup and restoration procedures
- Disaster recovery priorities
- Tabletop exercises

## Frameworks & Standards

- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2

- NIST Digital Identity Guidelines
- NICE Workforce Framework
- PCI DSS
- GDPR
